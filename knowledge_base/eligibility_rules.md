# Loan Eligibility and Appropriateness Rules

## Lending suitability principles
- The borrower should demonstrate stable income and repayment capacity.
- The debt burden should remain within a prudent threshold to avoid repayment stress.
- The credit profile must support the requested amount and tenure.
- The requested amount should align with stated affordability and financial goals.

## Practical assessment factors
- Credit score
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Existing EMI commitments
- Account stability and transaction history
- Employment continuity and income consistency
