# KYC and Identity Verification Guidance

## Purpose
KYC is essential to protect the financial system, mitigate fraud, and verify the identity and address of the customer.

## Minimum controls
- Validate identity, address, and occupation details.
- Confirm that the customer has a legitimate source of income.
- Use document verification and digital proof when permitted by regulation.
- Maintain a clear audit trail for all verification decisions.

## References
- RBI KYC guidelines and master directions.
- Government identity and address verification frameworks.
- Banking operations and compliance manuals.

## Risk outcomes
Applications with inconsistent names, mismatched addresses, or unsupported income sources should be flagged for manual review.
