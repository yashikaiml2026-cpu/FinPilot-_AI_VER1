# Personal Loan Policy Summary

## Overview
Loan policies must balance affordability, repayment capacity, and customer suitability. The bank should assess income, existing liabilities, repayment history, and risk profile before approval.

## Key policy guidance
- The proposed EMI should remain affordable relative to net monthly income.
- FOIR should remain within prudent levels for the risk category.
- Repayment capacity must be evaluated using debt-to-income ratio and monthly obligations.
- Existing loans and credit usage should be understood before increasing exposure.

## References
- Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines on digital lending and prudential customer protection.
- Personal loan affordability frameworks from banking policy documents.
- Internal risk management and credit evaluation standards.

## Decision principle
If the monthly repayment burden is high relative to income or there is evidence of repayment stress, the application should be escalated for additional review or documentation.
