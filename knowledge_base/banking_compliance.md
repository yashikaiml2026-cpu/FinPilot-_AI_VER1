# Banking Compliance Framework

## Core compliance expectations
- Ensure data privacy and consent for customer financial information.
- Maintain secure handling of account information and application records.
- Confirm that loan offers are explained clearly and that disclosures are not misleading.
- Record all assumptions, decisions, and reviews for auditability.

## Operational principle
The bank must align its lending workflows with regulatory requirements, customer protection standards, and internal governance policies.
