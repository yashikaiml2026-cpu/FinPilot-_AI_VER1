# Risk Assessment Framework

## Risk review dimensions
- Credit risk: repayment behavior and default tendency.
- Income risk: stability of employment and earnings.
- Leverage risk: existing debt obligations and monthly burden.
- Operational risk: documentation quality and process compliance.

## Risk moderation
Low-risk applications typically have stronger savings behavior, healthy income to repayment ratios, and manageable obligations. Higher-risk files should receive stronger review and documentation.
