# Customer Service and Communication Standards

## Service principles
- Communicate in a professional and transparent manner.
- Explain the reason for requests for information and documentation.
- Provide a clear summary of approved, pending, or declined decisions.
- Offer a path for escalation when the customer has questions.

## Best practice
When a customer requests a loan, the bank should explain the basis for approval, likely EMI, and required documents before any final commitment is made.
